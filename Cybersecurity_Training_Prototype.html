Welcome to Your Personalised Cybersecurity Training

A tailored, evidence-informed learning platform designed for university students.

University students face distinctive online risks, from targeted phishing emails to account credential compromise. Traditional awareness programmes often deliver generic, one-size-fits-all materials that can be unengaging or misaligned with individual needs.

This prototype tailors your training pathway based on a brief initial diagnostic. Instead of requiring you to complete every module in a fixed order, it prioritises topics where supportive guidance is most beneficial.

How It Works

  • Step 1: Initial Assessment — 6 short questions covering core cyber safety topics.
  • Step 2: Recommended Pathway — An adapted order of 6 concise training modules based on your answers.
  • Step 3: Interactive Learning — Scenario-based practice and authoritative security guidance.
  • Step 4: Adaptation — Dynamic reassessment updates your pathway as you complete modules.

If evaluating as part of a study, enter your assigned code. Otherwise, leave blank for anonymous use.

Privacy & Data Protection Statement

This research prototype operates securely. It does not request your real name, email, student ID, or passwords. All research evaluation responses remain strictly anonymous.

Note: Starting this assessment begins a training session within the prototype. Participant information and consent for the research evaluation are administered separately.

Research Note on Learning Gain
